The collisionally induced electron current and heat flow of a quasineutral plasma crossed by a drifting fast-ion
population: the simple-algebraic closure of Appelbe et al., Phys. Plasmas 26, 102704 (2019), the kinetic Fokker-Planck
solver behind it, and the drivers that reproduce every result of the paper.
